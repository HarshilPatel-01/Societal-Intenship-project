Institutional Baseline

About the Field Area

GEC Valsad covers 25 acres along Tithal Road, Valsad (PIN 396001), serving 1,200+ students and 120 staff. Abundant solar irradiance of 5.2 to 5.6 kWh/m²/day offers premier rooftop solar viability over 300+ sunny days.

Baseline Conditions

  • Electrical infrastructure from the 1980s with aged distribution busbars.
  • Machinery 15-20 yrs old with degraded IE1 induction motors.
  • 10,500 sq ft concrete rooftop completely unutilized with no solar.
  • 34% campus street lights non-functional; balance use 250W HPSV lamps.
  • No PM register; 54,376 kWh/month consumption with 35-40% avoidable waste.
Methodology

Community Diagnostic Study

50+ Staff Interviews

1-on-1 interviews with faculty, workshop superintendents, and lab staff capturing machine faults.

Student Questionnaires

Multi-department student cohort surveys on workshop lighting satisfaction and electrical safety perception.

Photometric Lux Audits

Digital Lux meter recorded <120 lux at 42 task benches vs IS 3646 mandate of 300–500 lux.

Clamp-On Analysers

Logged lagging power factor of 0.72 and severe motor idling running power dissipation.

Megger Earth Tests

Earth tester logged 4.2–8.1 Ω grounding resistance against IS 3043 (<1.0 Ω limit).

Observational Audits

Day/night surveillance of uninsulated lines, streetlight cycles, and machine idling.

Critical Deficits

Problem Statement

Critical

P-01: Safety Non-Compliance

Untapped

P-03: Zero Renewables

10,500 sq ft RCC roof completely vacant; ₹7–8.5 Lakhs/yr foregone; 100% grid dependence with ~530 t CO₂/yr.

Academic Reflection

Conclusion & Core Competencies

The 15-day GTU Societal Internship (BE05000011) at Government Engineering College, Valsad establishes a validated engineering blueprint for campus transformation. Combining mechanical safety retrofits (IS 11016), chemical earthing (IS 3043), and a 75 kWp rooftop solar plant avoids ~200 tonnes of CO₂ annually, slashes power bills by ₹17.3–23.1 Lakhs/yr, and secures 1,200+ students with a 2.5–3.5 year blended payback.

1. Safety Engineering

IS 11016 machine risk analysis and fail-safe interlock design.

2. Power & Earthing

Megger insulation measurement and IS 3043 chemical earthing design.

3. Solar PV Engineering

GHI irradiance modeling, string matching, and DGVCL net-metering.

4. Energy Auditing

Annexure 4 load disaggregation, clamp logging, and PF penalty math.

5. Techno-Economics

Table 10 capital budgeting, discounted payback, and sensitivity modeling.

6. Project Management

15-day WBS structuring, RACI governance, and risk triage.

7. Stakeholder Dialogue

Surveys with 50+ staff and 1,200+ students translated into engineering specs.
