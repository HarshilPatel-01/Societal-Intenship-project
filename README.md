## 🚀 Features of the Web Application

- **Self-Contained & Responsive:** Single-file production-grade architecture (`index.html`) with embedded styling and vanilla ES6+ scripts.
- **Theme Switcher:** Automatic OS color scheme detection with instant toggle between Ivory Luxury and Midnight Navy themes.
- **Interactive Data Visualizations:** 5 dynamic charts powered by Chart.js (Campus Energy Breakdown, Monthly Department Consumption, Budget vs Annual Savings, Payback Timeline, and Survey Feedback).
- **Interactive Solar & ROI Calculator:** Dynamic live sensitivity calculator calculating annual energy yield, net cost after subsidy, payback period, and CO₂ savings.
- **Field Gallery with In-Memory Preview:** Interactive lightbox with client-side image upload capability (FileReader API).
- **Detailed Compliance & RACI Matrix:** Machine safety guards (IS 11016:1984), electrical earthing standards (IS 3043), WBS, and interactive RACI responsibility matrix.
- **Print & PDF Ready:** Integrated print stylesheets (`@media print`) allowing one-click PDF generation for reports.
